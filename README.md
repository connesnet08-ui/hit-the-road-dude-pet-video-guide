# Hit the Road Dude pet video guide

A practical guide to making the white-pickup meme with one photo of a pet. Published and maintained by [Hit the Road Pets](https://hittheroadpets.com/). This is a product-maintained guide, with links to the original creator and alternative workflows.

Reviewed: October 21, 2026.

## Find the original first

The meme comes from an August 2025 fishing-spot confrontation posted by [@bird28thegr8 on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@bird28thegr8/video/7541156735361289485). It is also called State Property, Private Property and Lock ’Em Up. The complete recording is about 70 seconds; remakes often use a short excerpt. [Know Your Meme documents the source and remixes](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/lock-em-up-state-property).

Showing a viral reference clip does not establish that a tool produced that clip. Look for an example paired with its input photo.

## One-photo pet tutorial

1. Play a completed result with sound before choosing a tool.
2. Pick a sharp, well-lit photo of one pet. Keep eyes, ears, muzzle and some upper body visible. Use a photo you have permission to use.
3. Open the studio, add the photo and inspect the preview.
4. Check the total starting purchase, video format, credit expiry and failed-job policy.
5. Generate once and follow that job; avoid submitting the same request while it is pending.
6. Watch the whole result, then download the MP4 before its temporary link expires.

## Current Hit the Road Pets facts

- Input: one JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB.
- Intended output: approximately 15 seconds, 480p, portrait 9:16 MP4 with sound.
- Process: prepared pickup scene and source-guided reference editing; no prompt or reference-video upload required from the customer.
- One-time packs: $5.99 for one video, $14.99 for three, $22.99 for five. No automatic subscription.
- Each video uses 150 credits; purchased credits are valid for 365 days.
- Confirmed failed or rejected jobs return reserved credits. This differs from refunding the pack payment.
- The public cat test has restored original audio; motion and lip sync still differ from the reference. New orders use source-guided native sound, with possible differences in words and timing.

Always check the current [pricing](https://hittheroadpets.com/pricing), [sample](https://hittheroadpets.com/examples/silver-tabby) and studio availability before buying.
